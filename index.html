How this works

Four sections. The first three run in order, Habits sits on its own.

1
🌀 Dump
Type whatever is rattling around, one thought at a time. Don't organise, don't judge, just empty it out. Clear signals get sorted for you as they land: "today" goes Urgent, "every morning" becomes a Habit, and "milk" joins Grocery. Category kind controls behavior: list categories route to Lists, habit categories route to Habits, and priority categories stay in the task flow. Everything else stays grey on purpose, because a wrong guess costs you more than no guess.
2
🗂️ Sort
Anything that came back grey waits in Needs a home at the top. Tap a bucket, or use Send all if you just want them somewhere. Below that, every bucket shows what landed in it and why. Drag between zones or use the dropdown to correct anything. Corrections teach the sorting, so the same kind of thought lands better next time. Open What the icons mean for the full key.
3
✅ The list
Interactive is for working: check things off, tap any line to edit it, reorder with the arrows, hide what's finished. Today's habits sit at the top, and ticking one logs the day. Static is for leaving: a clean sheet you can print or copy as plain text to paste into an email. This step covers tasks only, so twelve grocery items can't bury your three real priorities. A reminder chip appears at the top when a list has items waiting. If you'd rather print everything on one page, tick Include my lists in the static view and they get appended after your tasks.
🛒
Lists
Grocery and Shopping sit outside the numbered steps for the same reason Habits do: a list item has no priority. Milk isn't urgent or important, it's just something to grab. Pick a list with the pills at the top, add straight to it, then use Interactive while you shop and Static to print or copy the list to take with you. When you're home, Clear checked sweeps everything you got in one go. Anything you type in step 1 that reads like food lands in Grocery on its own.
🔁
Habits
Habits aren't tasks, so they don't live in the three steps. A task gets finished and leaves. A habit repeats forever, so it gets a streak instead of a checkbox. Tap any day in the row to log it, change how often it should happen with the dropdown, and watch the last four weeks in the dot strip underneath. Missing today doesn't break your streak until tomorrow. Add one here directly, or type something like "stretch every morning" in step 1 and it lands here on its own.
🗝️ The icons

Everything saves as you go. The pill at the top tells you where. Profile has export and import if you want a copy.

Manage categories

Priority buckets answer "when do I do this." A habit bucket is different: anything sorted there becomes a tracked routine instead of a task.
